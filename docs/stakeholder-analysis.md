# SWE303 — Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Interest / need | Influence | Engagement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Bhutan Olympic Committee / national organizer | National oversight, tournament setup, reliable final tables | High | Review tournament rules and approve result-correction policy |
| Dzongkhag sports administrators | Register local teams and coordinate venues without cross-dzongkhag edits | High | Validate registration and permissions workflows |
| Match officials | Enter live scores and finalize only their assigned matches | High | Walk through score and finalization flow |
| Team managers | Register teams and check fixtures and standings | Medium | Validate team forms and match information |
| Players and supporters | Read public fixture and result information | Low | Gather usability feedback on public views |
| Course lecturers / evaluators | Assess architecture, testing evidence, scope control, and project documentation | High | Review the scope baseline and traceability artifacts |
| Student development team | Deliver and maintain the semester MVP | High | Weekly progress review and risk/change log |

## Communication plan

- Weekly progress checkpoint: completed work, next work, active risks, and decisions needed.
- Architecture or scope changes: record the request, impact, decision, and approver in `change-control.md`.
- Release/demo: publish run instructions, known limits, and the tested build identifier.
- Defects: record severity, reproduction steps, owner, and resolution evidence in `defect-log.md`.
