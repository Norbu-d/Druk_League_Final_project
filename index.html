What you can run

One place for the whole tournament

Live scoring

The assigned match official updates the score in real time. Once finalised, the result is locked; only an audited admin correction can change it.

Automatic standings

Every final result updates the table: three points for a win, one for a draw.

Fixtures & venues

Round-robin, double round-robin and knockout fixtures, with venue clash checks.

Teams & registration

Teams are registered for each tournament, and only approved teams are scheduled into fixtures.

Notices & reports

Team managers are notified when a result is final, and standings and fixtures export as CSV or JSON.

How it works

From sign-up to final table

  1. 01

    Create a tournament

    Set the season, dzongkhag and fixture format.

  2. 02

    Register teams

    Teams from across the dzongkhags join the tournament.

  3. 03

    Generate fixtures

    Matches are scheduled with venues and kick-off times.

  4. 04

    Score live

    Officials update scores and finalise results.

  5. 05

    Publish standings

    Teams, officials and fans follow the updated table.
